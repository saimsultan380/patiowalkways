   Frequently Asked Questions
6. What outdoor services do you offer in Charlotte?
   We provide complete outdoor improvement services, including patios and walkways, paver installation, concrete and paver driveways, retaining and seating walls, steps, outdoor kitchens, fire pits, fireplaces, pergolas, decks, landscape lighting, drainage, irrigation, grading, landscaping, fencing, water features, and commercial hardscaping.
7. What types of patios can you install?
   We install a wide range of custom patios to suit different styles, properties, and budgets. Options include paver patios, natural stone patios such as flagstone, bluestone, and slate, brick patios, poured concrete, stamped concrete, colored concrete, travertine, and porcelain paver patios.
8. Are pavers better than concrete for a patio or driveway?
   Both can be excellent choices, depending on your property and goals. Pavers offer flexible design options and individual pieces can be replaced if damaged, while concrete provides a smooth, versatile surface and can be stamped or colored. We can help you compare materials based on appearance, durability, maintenance, drainage, and budget.
   Charlotte contractors commonly highlight pavers as a strong option where ground movement and future repairs are concerns.
9. How much does a patio or paver project cost in Charlotte?
   The cost depends on the size of the project, selected materials, site preparation, grading, drainage, accessibility, design complexity, and additional features such as retaining walls, steps, fire pits, or outdoor kitchens. Rather than giving every project the same price, we assess your property and provide an estimate based on your specific project.
10. How long does it take to install a patio or walkway?
    Project timelines vary depending on the size, material, site conditions, excavation, drainage requirements, and design. A straightforward patio or walkway may take several days, while larger outdoor living projects with walls, kitchens, fire features, or extensive grading can take considerably longer. We provide a project timeline before work begins.
    Charlotte competitors commonly cite several days for straightforward paver work, with larger and more complex projects taking longer.
11. Do you install walkways and garden paths?
    Yes. We install custom walkways, garden paths, and front entry walkways using materials such as pavers, brick, natural stone, and concrete. We can design the layout to complement your home, landscaping, patio, driveway, and overall outdoor space.
12. Do you provide drainage and grading for outdoor projects?
    Yes. Proper grading and drainage are important parts of many outdoor projects, particularly around patios, walkways, driveways, retaining walls, and landscaped areas. We can address drainage concerns and grade the site appropriately to help manage water and protect your outdoor improvements.
    This is particularly relevant to Charlotte projects because local contractors frequently identify site preparation, drainage, grading, and soil conditions as important considerations.

    Yes. We install retaining walls, seating walls, and stone or paver steps as part of outdoor hardscaping projects. These features can improve usability, define different areas of your yard, manage changes in elevation, and add visual interest to patios and landscaped spaces.
